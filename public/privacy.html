Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 14, 2026

Agnus ("we", "our", or "the app") is a pattern-matching puzzle game. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

Information We Collect

Account Information: If you choose to create an account, we collect your email address and a username you choose. You may also sign in with Google, in which case we receive your email address from Google.

Game Data: We store your puzzle progress, solve times, achievements, and game preferences. This data is stored locally on your device and, if you have an account, synced to our cloud service.

User-Generated Content: If you create mosaic designs, these are stored on our servers and may be shared publicly if you submit them for review.

Profile Information: You may optionally set a profile picture, which is stored on our servers.

Analytics: We use Vercel Analytics to collect anonymous usage data such as page views and performance metrics. No personally identifiable information is collected through analytics.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve the game experience
  • To sync your progress across devices when signed in
  • To enable social features (friends, co-op play, leaderboards)
  • To display your username and profile to other players in social features
  • To send you in-app notifications (friend requests, co-op invites)

Data Storage

Your data is stored using Firebase (Google Cloud Platform). Game data is stored locally on your device using browser/app local storage and, when signed in, synced to Firebase Realtime Database.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. Your username and profile picture are visible to other signed-in users through social features (friends list, leaderboards, co-op sessions). Your email address is only used for account authentication and friend lookups.

Account Deletion

You can delete your account at any time from the app settings. This will permanently remove your account, all game data, friends, mosaics, and any other data associated with your account from our servers. Local data on your device will also be cleared. This action cannot be undone.

Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures provided by Firebase and Google Cloud Platform to protect your data. Access to user data is restricted through Firebase Security Rules that ensure users can only access their own data.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at the email address associated with this app's App Store listing.

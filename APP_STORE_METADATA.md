## Description
Agnus is a pattern-matching puzzle game that challenges you to decode hidden patterns and fill in the blanks. With eight unique game modes, daily puzzles, and a rich progression system, there's always a new challenge waiting.

GAME MODES
- Easy, Medium & Hard — Classic grids with increasing complexity
- Blind — No color hints, shapes only
- Spin — The pattern rotates as you solve
- Mosaic — Epic 25x25 grids divided into tile-based puzzles
- Daily — A fresh puzzle every day to keep your streak alive
- Cascade — A progressive difficulty run from 3x3 up to 9x9

FEATURES
- 300+ handcrafted puzzles across all modes
- Daily puzzles with streak tracking
- 27+ achievements across 5 categories
- 17 unlockable themes (seasonal and achievement-based)
- Cloud sync — pick up where you left off on any device
- Co-op multiplayer — solve puzzles with friends in real time
- Mosaic Creator — design and share your own 25x25 puzzles
- Friends system with activity tracking and leaderboards
- Gold, Silver & Bronze medals based on your performance

Test your logic. Find the pattern. Fill in the blanks.
